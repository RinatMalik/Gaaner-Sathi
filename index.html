My takes

While Record me is on, each play is a take. Play one back against the guide to hear where you were off. Takes stay in this browser.

    How your singing is scored
    What is checkedWhether you sing the same note the app is playing, at the same time.
    Each noteWhile a note plays, the app listens to you many times a second. Right on the note gets full marks. The further off you are, the fewer marks, and a little over half a semitone off gets none. The note’s score is the average.
    What lowers itSinging a little high or low, or a different note. Coming in late, still on the note before. Moving to the next note too early.
    What doesn’tTaking a breath or pausing, and the sounds in words that have no tune, like the start of স ত ক প. Sliding up or down into a note (a meend). Notes you don’t sing at all: they show grey and are left out. Rests.
    ColoursGreen: in tune. Amber: close. Red: off. Grey: not scored (sliding into a note, or no note playing).
    The numbersEach line’s score is in its number box. The number under the song is your score for the whole run, and is saved in Progress.
    See where it went wrongTap a line to open it. Under each note: how high or low you were (+12 is a little high, −8 a little low; a semitone is 100), and how late you reached it, if you did.
    Being lateUnder the song it says how far behind the music you usually are, to help you sing on time. The app never moves your singing to hide it.
    Your equipmentHeadphones and microphones take a moment to pass sound along. Measure it once with Measure the delay, under the song’s title, so you are not marked late for it.
    OctavesThe right note in a higher or lower octave counts, unless you tick Octave must match, at the top under the lines to practise.
    Not scoredRounds where the guide plays for you to listen first.
    AfterwardsReport, under the song after a run and beside each session in Progress, opens a page on how it went: what went well, what to work on, your timing and each note. For AI saves everything about it as a file an AI such as Claude or ChatGPT can analyse: the tune the app heard, not a recording of you.

    A rewaz runs exercises and songs one after another, each with its own lines, rounds, tempo and guide, so you can sing without touching the screen. Each one goes into your practice log.

    How to type your diary notation
    Beat boxesEach box is one beat, laid out by the taal. Type the note and a space to go to the next box, or paste a line such as সা রা গা মা into a box to fill a box each. sa re ga works too. Enter starts a new line; Backspace in an empty box removes it. The first beat of each part of the taal is coloured: sam, tali or khali. At the end of each line, its buttons play just that line, play from that line to the end of the song, and remove it. Tap a line (anywhere but a box) or its number to select it; drag a line by its number to move it (or press ↑ ↓ on the number), and select several lines first to move them together. To copy lines, select them and press Copy; then select the line they should go after and press Paste, as often as you like, in this song or another. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V on a number do the same.
    WordsPaste a line of the song's words into the word box under its first note: they are split into syllables, one under each note, with - where a note is held, on into the next lines. Or type them, a space between beats: আ মার সো নার typed in the first box fills four. Correct any syllable by typing over it. Enter in the words adds another row of them (a second verse).
    Two notes in one beatType them together, with no space: সারা, SR or sare. They share the beat equally. To add a note to a box that has one, tap at the end of it (or press →) and type.
    One beat (matra)Each group between spaces is one beat: সা রা গা is three beats.

    Fix a wrong octaveChoose the beat's box, then press ↑ Higher or ↓ Lower. In Free text, tap the beat in the preview (shift-tap a second beat to select a run) or select notes in the text; there Alt + ↑ and Alt + ↓ do the same while typing.

    Silencex. The note before it fades out gently.
    Two or more notes in one beatType them together with no space: পধা, পাধা or PD. Uneven: S-R gives Sa two thirds and Re one third. Or choose a beat (its box, or in Free text the beat in the preview) and press Join to merge it with the next one, or Split to separate its notes.
    Bars| । ॥ are for reading only. Timing comes from the beats.
    LyricsA line starting with > under its notes line, one group per beat. Use ০ for a held vowel.
    HeadingsA line starting with #, such as # অন্তরা. A tempo for that section alone can go in it: # অন্তরা · tempo 110.
    A taal for one section (তাল ফেরতা)Name the taal in the section's heading: # অন্তরা · কাহারবা. In the beat boxes, pick it beside the heading. That section plays, and is checked, in that taal, starting on its sam; the sections after it go back to the song's taal. If the singing comes in after sam, put x (silence) for the beats before it.
