About this song

A rewaz runs exercises and songs one after another, each with its own lines, rounds, tempo and guide, so you can sing without touching the screen. Each one goes into your practice log.

    How to type your diary notation
    Beat boxesEach box is one beat, laid out by the taal. Type the note and a space to go to the next box, or paste a line such as সা রা গা মা into a box to fill a box each. sa re ga works too. Enter starts a new line; Backspace in an empty box removes it. The words go in the boxes under the notes.
    One beat (matra)Each group between spaces is one beat: সা রা গা is three beats.
    Notesসা রা গা মা পা ধা না or S R G M P D N
    Komalঋা জ্ঞা দা ণা or r g d n
    Tivra Maহ্মা or M#
    Upper octave (তার)র্সা (রেফ) or S'
    Lower octave (মন্দ্র).না or ন্ with হসন্ত, or .N
    Fix a wrong octaveTap the beat in the preview, or select notes in the box, then press ↑ Higher or ↓ Lower. Shift-tap a second beat to select a run. While typing, Alt + ↑ and Alt + ↓ do the same.
