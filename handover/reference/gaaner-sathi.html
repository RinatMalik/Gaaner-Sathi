How to type your diary notation
One beat (matra)Each group between spaces is one beat: সা রা গা is three beats.
Notesসা রা গা মা পা ধা না or S R G M P D N
Komalঋা জ্ঞা দা ণা or r g d n
Tivra Maহ্মা or M#
Upper octave (তার)র্সা (রেফ) or S'
Lower octave (মন্দ্র).না or ন্ with হসন্ত, or .N
Fix a wrong octaveTap the beat in the preview, or select notes in the box, then press ↑ Higher or ↓ Lower. Shift-tap a second beat to select a run. While typing, Alt + ↑ and Alt + ↓ do the same.
Hold the note-া or -

Two or more notes in one beatType them together with no space: পধা, পাধা or PD. Uneven: S-R gives Sa two thirds and Re one third. Or tap a beat in the preview and press Join to merge it with the next one, or Split to separate its notes.
Bars| । ॥ are for reading only. Timing comes from the beats.
LyricsA line starting with > under its notes line, one group per beat. Use ০ for a held vowel.
HeadingsA line starting with #, such as # অন্তরা
