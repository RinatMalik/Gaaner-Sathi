Once you have picked

  • The chosen logo replaces the name at the top left of every view, by day and by night, with tagline 16 under it. It is drawn as a picture, so the app loads no new typeface.
  • Anything can still be tuned: the angle, how deep the water comes, how many waves, their colours.
  • The typeface is chosen on its own, whenever you like; the waves are fitted to whichever it is.
  • The tagline is a line of text: changing it later is a one-line edit.
  • The app’s icon (the browser tab and a phone’s home screen) is made from the logo afterwards.
