Twenty logos for the top-left corner

তরঙ্গিণী is a river: word for word, one full of waves. These logos play on that, and on what the app already is: the harmonium, the tanpura, the river and its boats, the brass play button and the diary sheet with its red margin.

Each is drawn the way the app's header would show it, by day and by night, with its app icon at full size and at the size of a browser tab. Pick one logo and one tagline; the preview below shows them together.

The logos

Nine of them (2, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 17, 18 and 20) use a Bengali typeface the app does not load today. Whichever you choose, its lettering goes into the app as a drawing, so the app loads nothing new and stays as light as it is.

Twenty taglines

The short line under the name, where the header now says “harmonium and rhythm from your own notation”. Twelve in English and eight in Bengali; please check the Bengali reads naturally to you.

Once you have picked

  • The logo replaces the name at the top left of every view, drawn sharp at any size and in both colour schemes.
  • Its icon becomes the browser tab's icon, and the icon a phone shows when the app is added to the home screen.
  • Any of them can be adjusted: colours, the typeface, the Latin “Torongini” line kept or dropped.
